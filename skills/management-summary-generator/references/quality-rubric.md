# Management Summary Quality & Evidence Rubric

## Required sections

| Section | Expected content | When unavailable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Executive takeaway | What changed, why it matters and management implication | State missing context |
| Key findings | Three to five sourced insights | Provide fewer, never fabricate |
| Decisions | Agreed, requested or proposed decisions with explicit status | “Not established in the supplied material” |
| Risks & dependencies | Material obstacles and implications evident in sources | Same explicit absence statement |
| Actions | Action, owner and due date when actually recorded | “Unspecified” or leave absent |
| Open questions | Missing evidence or sponsor choices | Distinguish no questions identified from missing information |

## Evidence and quality controls

- **Evidence:** Reference source ID and location for each finding, decision, risk and action. A reference alone does not prove semantic support; inspect the underlying passage.
- **Figures:** Reconcile percentages, amounts, baselines, targets, dates and units exactly. State when a figure is illustrative or disputed.
- **Attribution:** Never convert “team proposes” into “steering committee approved”.
- **Tone:** Concise, neutral, insight-led and free of unsupported executive optimism.
- **Confidentiality:** Use only authorized sources and destinations; do not expose client content in public demos.
- **Unsupported conclusions:** Remove, label as inference, or request supporting evidence.
- **Failure mode:** If source material is thin, provide a coverage/gap note rather than a fabricated summary.

## Suggested evaluation criteria

Score each from 0–2 (0 absent/incorrect, 1 partial, 2 complete): factual support, coverage, traceability, decision/action accuracy, clarity and handling of uncertainty. Require reviewer approval for client-facing outputs.
